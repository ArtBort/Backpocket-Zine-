Backpocket site desk

Edit the zine site.

Changes are saved straight to your GitHub repository, and the live site updates a minute or two later. ← Back to the site

1 · Connect to GitHub

Only someone with your GitHub access token can save changes here. The token stays in this browser only. Use your own device, and press “Disconnect” on a shared one.

2 · Your site: click anything to edit it

How it works: this is your real site. All text has an orange dotted outline: hover to see its name, click and type. Images (the cover art and every gallery photo) also have an outline: click an empty part of one to replace it. Nothing goes live until you press Publish changes.

3 · Colours

Changes show in the preview straight away.

4 · Fonts

Pick a font for the big headlines and one for the normal text, or upload your own font file (.woff2, .woff, .ttf or .otf). The preview updates straight away.

“Google Fonts” options are loaded from Google’s servers when someone visits the site. Standard fonts and uploaded files are not. Only upload fonts you have the right to use on a website.

5 · Images

Files over about 8 MB load slowly for visitors. iPhone HEIC photos are converted to JPG because browsers can’t show HEIC.

Cover picture (top of the page)

Replaces the paper-collage cover artwork. Remove it to bring the artwork back.

Gallery photos

Use the arrows to reorder. To swap a photo, click it in the preview.

6 · Sign-up forms & donations

Both forms (zine sign-up and artist submissions) send an email to the address you choose. They use the free service Web3Forms, which allows up to 250 emails a month.

  1. Go to web3forms.com, type the email address that should receive sign-ups, and press “Create Access Key”.
  2. They email you a key. Paste it below.

A paypal.me link gives people buttons for €3, €5, €10 and “other amount”. Any other PayPal link gives one “Contribute with PayPal” button. Leave it empty to show no donation ask.

7 · Page title and search description

The title shows on the browser tab. The description is what search engines show under the title.
