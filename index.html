A pocket-sized practice

Made by hand.
Made to wander.

Backpocket is its own little world, alongside the artist’s wider practice. It starts with collage and stays open to whatever medium, memory, or question belongs in the next page.

01 / Collect

Save the fragments.

Images, textures, phrases, odd observations: a starting point is allowed to be small.

02 / Connect

Follow the thread.

Let research and experiments shift the idea. Nothing needs to be settled on the first pass.

03 / Share

Send it out.

A zine turns a private pile of things into something another person can hold and respond to.
